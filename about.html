01 / OUR HOUSE

Made for the
in-between.

The Still began with a question: what happens when a hotel stops trying to fill every moment?

We kept the rooms calm, the materials tactile and the service close at hand. The result is a place for the hours between arrival and departure — coffee before the city wakes, an afternoon with nowhere to be, a quiet return after dark.
