About The Still

A hotel for
slower hours.

We built The Still around a simple idea: a good hotel should give you back some of your time.

The Still / Central City

01 / The idea

The building
keeps time.

Most days move quickly enough. We wanted to create a place where the room, the light and the spaces around it encourage a different rhythm.

Nothing here is designed to demand your attention. The architecture stays calm. The service stays close. The best parts of the day are allowed to remain small.

02 / What we believe

Stay for
the feeling.

The Still is less about adding more and more about removing what gets in the way of a good stay.

01TIME

Let the day stretch

We leave room for mornings, afternoons and evenings to become their own thing.

02SPACE

Quiet by design

Natural light, considered materials and rooms that never feel overworked.

03CARE

Close, not intrusive

Thoughtful hospitality that notices what matters and leaves the rest alone.

04PLACE

Part of the city

A calm base for going out, coming back and finding your own route through the day.
